YOUTUBE PACKAGING BRIEF — TITLE + THUMBNAIL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

I need help packaging a YouTube video — title and thumbnail
concept. Use this framework. Ask me clarifying questions
before producing anything, especially about the topic, the
core tension, and what my niche's competitors look like.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CORE PRINCIPLE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Package first, film second. If the title + thumbnail pair
isn't compelling, the video idea isn't strong enough.
This is the workflow MrBeast and Ali Abdaal use — title
gets written before anything is shot.

The Triangle: Title + Thumbnail + Hook must tell the same
story with escalating tension. Title creates the curiosity
gap. Thumbnail visualizes it without resolving it. Hook
(first 10s of video) validates both.

If any of the three doesn't fit, the package is broken.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THUMBNAIL RULES (2026)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. ONE focal point. Max 2–3 visual elements total.
   (face + object + text, OR face + background)

2. THREE words of text or fewer. Mobile is 60%+ of
   viewing — anything more becomes unreadable noise.
   Thumbnail text complements the title, never repeats it.
     Title: "5 Budget Lighting Setups"
     Thumbnail text: "UNDER $50"  (not "BUDGET LIGHTING")

3. HIGH contrast. Bright subject on dark background, or
   complementary color pairs. Test in grayscale — if you
   can't tell the focal point, contrast is wrong.

4. REAL face beats AI face. AI thumbnails get initial CTR
   but lose viewer satisfaction over time, and the
   algorithm now measures both. Use real micro-expressions.

5. PATTERN INTERRUPT the niche. Search the topic on
   YouTube, screenshot the top 12 thumbnails. If everyone
   uses red, go neon green or deep black. Stand out from
   the feed, not just look "good."

6. EMOTION on the face — surprise, curiosity, intensity.
   Neutral faces blend in. Expressive faces lift CTR
   20–30% (VidIQ data).

Specs: 1280×720 minimum, 1920×1080 recommended.
Under 2MB to be safe (YouTube now allows 50MB on desktop
but mobile uploads still glitch above 2MB).

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TITLE RULES (2026)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Technical:
  - Under 60 characters (mobile truncates after that)
  - Keyword in first 5 words (algorithm weight)
  - 1–2 ALL CAPS words MAX for emphasis
  - Use "I" and "you" — personal pronouns lift CTR
  - Generate 5–10 options, pick the strongest
  - If it needs a second read to "get it," it's too complex

Title formulas that work for educational content:

  COMPRESSION (time-bounded promise)
    "Learn German in 20 Minutes"
    "Give Me 9 Minutes, and I'll Save You 10 Years"

  AUTHORITY + REVEAL
    "Former Google Engineer Reveals How Search Actually Works"
    "Neuroscientist Explains Why You Can't Remember Dreams"

  CONTRARIAN / PATTERN BREAK
    "Everything You Know About Sleep Is Wrong"
    "Why Your Productivity System Is Actually Hurting You"

  CURIOSITY GAP
    "The One React Mistake That's Slowing Down Your App"
    "What 99% of Devs Get Wrong About useEffect"

  MISTAKES / REGRET (loss aversion = 2x stronger than gain)
    "5 Mistakes I Made in My First Year of Coding"
    "Things I Wish I Knew Before Starting With AI Tooling"

  NUMBER LIST
    "7 React Patterns That Will Make You a Senior Dev"

STACK formulas for stronger CTR:
  "7 Things I Wish I Knew Before Starting YouTube"
  = Number List + Regret. Stacked titles outperform
  single-formula by 15–25%.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE TITLE + THUMBNAIL + HOOK TRIANGLE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Worked example:

  TITLE:     "Why Your Dreams Feel So Weird
              (It's Not Your Subconscious)"

  THUMBNAIL: Confused face + brain diagram with the
             prefrontal cortex crossed out + text
             "EDITOR OFFLINE"

  HOOK:      "Why do your dreams feel so weird?
              You're in your childhood house, but it's
              also your office, and your dead grandma
              is there but she's also your boss…"

Layers working together:
  - Title = curiosity gap + contrarian element
  - Thumbnail = visualizes the mechanism (brain editor)
    without giving away the answer
  - Hook = validates both within 5 seconds

The viewer must feel SATISFIED, not deceived, after
watching. If the triangle's promise isn't delivered in
the video, the algorithm punishes hard (high CTR + early
abandonment = net negative signal in 2026).

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CTR BENCHMARKS (so we know what we're targeting)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Subscription feed (loyal fans)     8–15%
  YouTube Search (high intent)       3–9%
  Home feed / Browse                 3–8%
  Suggested videos (hardest)         2–6%
  External (Twitter, Reddit)         10–20%

YouTube average is 4–5%. "Good" starts at 4%.
Below 2% on Search = title/topic mismatch.
Below 2% on Subscription feed = your fans don't
care about this topic.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
MISTAKES TO AVOID
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  - Title and thumbnail saying the same thing
    (wasted slot — they should layer, not duplicate)
  - Auto-generated thumbnails (custom = +60–70% CTR)
  - Clickbait that doesn't deliver in the video
  - 5+ words on thumbnail (unreadable on mobile)
  - More than one focal point on the thumbnail
  - Cluttered design — kill 3 elements if in doubt
  - Inconsistent style across videos (kills brand recall)
  - Filming first, packaging after (BIGGEST mistake)

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT I NEED FROM YOU
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Before producing anything, ask me:

  1. What's the video topic in one sentence?
  2. What's the core tension / misconception the video
     addresses? (If I don't have one, push back —
     without it, the package has no curiosity gap.)
  3. Who's the audience and what's their level?
  4. What does my niche's competition look like?
     (I can drop screenshots or describe it.)
  5. Do I have a face to use, or is this faceless?
  6. What's the one quotable line I want viewers to
     walk away with? (this often becomes the thumbnail
     text or part of the title)

Then deliver:

  A. 5–10 title options, each labeled with its formula
     (Compression / Authority / Contrarian / etc.)
     Pick the top 3 and explain why.

  B. 2–3 thumbnail concepts described in detail:
     - Focal point (face / object / scene)
     - Background style and color
     - Text overlay (3 words max)
     - The specific emotion / expression
     - How it pattern-interrupts the niche

  C. The full triangle for the top recommendation:
     Title + Thumbnail concept + Hook (first 10s of
     video script). Show how all three layer to tell
     one story.

  D. An A/B test plan — which 2 variants I should run
     against each other in YouTube Studio's built-in
     A/B test, and what hypothesis we're testing.
