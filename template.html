Hook

Open a curiosity gap in under 10 seconds. Pick one: question · counterintuitive · cold open · stakes · puzzle · promise · story.

Frame

The contract with the viewer. Stakes (why this matters) + scope (who it's for, what's covered, how long). 1–3 sentences, ~10–20 seconds.

Tension

Surface the misconception. What the viewer thinks they know vs what's actually true. Don't resolve yet — let the discomfort sit.
